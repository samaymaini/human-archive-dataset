Human Archive · Egocentric dataset

15,000 hours of people doing real work, seen through their own eyes

First-person video recorded inside working businesses across India: factories, warehouses, kitchens, farms, workshops, construction sites and shops. Captured with a head-mounted stereo camera and a multi-camera rig with wrist cameras, with 3D hand keypoints, camera pose, IMU and dense action annotations.

Category
The industry the business belongs to.
Environment type
The kind of venue within a category (for example, a cold storage within warehousing and logistics).
Distinct scene
A scene type at one business with at least five minutes of footage. The same kind of scene recorded at a different business counts as a new distinct scene.
Task description
The specific activity in a labelled segment (for example, "Weighing and bagging tomatoes"); the count is of unique descriptions.
Hours
Measured on delivered clip duration; shares are computed over hours.
