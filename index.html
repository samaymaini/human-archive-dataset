Human Archive Egocentric Dataset

15,000 hours of first-person video of people doing real work in real businesses across India, recorded with head-mounted stereo cameras and a multi-camera rig with wrist cameras, with 3D hand keypoints, camera pose, IMU and dense action annotations. This page breaks the dataset down by category, environment, scene and task.

Hours are measured on delivered clip duration. Category = industry category; environment = venue type within a category; scene = the work area shown; task = the activity performed. Shares are computed over hours.
